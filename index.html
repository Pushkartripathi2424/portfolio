A bit about me

I'm Pushkar, a B.Des student in UX/Interaction Design at Manipal University Jaipur. I'm drawn to products that solve everyday problems for people my age, like managing a first salary, keeping track of goals or meeting new people on campus.

I like testing ideas quickly with real people before polishing pixels, and I use AI tools to move faster from idea to prototype. Right now I'm working towards becoming a job-ready UI/UX designer and taking on freelance work.
