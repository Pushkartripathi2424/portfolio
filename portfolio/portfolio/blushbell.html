BlushBell

A proximity-based campus app that rings when someone nearby is your mutual match. Tested on the ground before writing a single line of code.

RoleFounder + UX Designer
StageConcept & validation
PlatformiOS first
CampusMUJ
45strangers in a live matching test
13–15pairs matched
13/15survey responses favourable

The problem

On a busy campus, students pass hundreds of people every day but rarely start a conversation with a stranger. Dating apps feel endless and impersonal, not local and in-the-moment.

The idea

Users set their gender, who they're interested in, and their "type". When someone nearby matches both ways, a bell rings on both phones. With 400+ students within 50–100 metres of each other on campus, the chance of a nearby match is real.

Process

  1. Survey of about 12 questions, plus "what you see around campus" questions so each person also reported on their friends. 15 responses, 13 favourable.
  2. On-ground matching test. Instead of building the app first, I ran the matching by hand with 45 students who were all strangers. 13–15 pairs formed.
  3. Platform decision: iOS first, because most students on campus use iPhones.
  4. Launch thinking: a fest-style campus launch to build hype, then scale campus by campus.

Result

A validated concept with real proof that the matching works. The next step is designing the iOS prototype.

What I learned: testing an idea by hand, cheaply and fast, gives you real evidence before you spend months building it.
